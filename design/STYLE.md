# Invitation style

Keep the scrolling watercolor invitation and its decorative motion. Guest-facing wording should be short and literal: “Save the date,” “Your details,” “Mailing address,” “Can you attend?” and “Submit.”

Do not add romantic slogans, story metaphors, heart ornaments, sentimental thank-you messages, or empty section preambles. The couple specifically rejected phrases such as “The best stories have you in them” and “A little note before the big day.”

Use Columbia-inspired details sparingly: Columbia Blue `#B9D9EB`, darker blue type for legibility, fine blue rules, blue butterflies. Keep the warm paper and airy watercolor woodland. Use a simple, elegant forest scene with soft trees, leafy branches, moss and ferns. Do not add tables, chairs, candles, place settings or banquet decorations. Keep the top-left corner free of the S & E monogram. Keep the opening free of crown ornaments; the user specifically rejected the crown above “Save the date.” Use an **S&E** favicon, with navy serif letters on Columbia Blue. The vector source is `dist/assets/monogram-se.svg`, with PNG/ICO fallbacks and an Apple touch icon.

Color reference: [Columbia University Visual Identity — Color](https://visualidentity.columbia.edu/content/color).

Use the nighttime waterfront photograph for the couple portrait. The user rejected the graduation portrait because only one person was wearing a gown.

Butterflies should follow gently rising and falling paths across the open space above the text as unchanged illustrations; keep the central information clear. Use very slow, ambient motion (roughly 64–82 seconds per crossing). Avoid straight, constant-speed crossings. Do not rotate them or animate the wings with scaling or perspective changes. Keep the scroll progress line over the painted background, as an unframed 3px blue line and 6px moving dot. No pill, container, border, numbers, or chevron; the user rejected the cream-backed control as inelegant. Group the weekday, June 20, year, and time into a balanced date composition.

The opening scroll instruction is plain text with an arrow below it. It must not be a button, look like a button, or respond to clicks.
