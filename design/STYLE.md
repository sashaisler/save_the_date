# Invitation style

Use one combined invitation view followed by one complete form. All invitation details must be visible together; all form questions must be present together with one Submit button. Use normal page scrolling, with no pinned scenes, scroll-driven fades, chapter progress bar, Next/Back buttons, or repeated scroll prompts.

Carry one continuous watercolor woodland backdrop through the invitation and form, with a nearly opaque paper wash behind the details and form, leaving only a very faint trace of the forest. Keep a short soft fade at the section entrance. On mobile, size the shared backdrop using a stable large viewport height so browser toolbar changes do not zoom the artwork while scrolling. Keep the desktop form within the existing artwork’s width and faded edges; narrow the form to fit rather than stretching the forest image. Separate Name and Mailing address with a fine, muted line (vertical on desktop, horizontal on phones). Keep the warm paper, Columbia Blue `#B9D9EB`, navy text, fine blue rules, and readable form text. Use dark navy labels and medium-weight headings, darker placeholders and optional notes; keep the Enter information below and registry links over one broad, softly fading paper wash so forest details do not compete with them. Do not put individual boxes or rectangular backings around either link. Names, date, time and venue use restrained serif typography. Keep the existing calendar and directions buttons comfortably sized.

Use the nighttime waterfront watercolor portrait. The graduation portrait was rejected because only one person wore a gown. Keep the artwork free of tables, chairs, candles and banquet decorations. Do not add a top-left monogram or crown ornament. The favicon stays S&E.

Use the original Columbia blue butterfly palette. Preserve the existing butterflies and petals: the same illustrations, scattered areas, paths and timings, with no wing scaling or perspective changes. Petals fall downward. Both continue to animate with Reduce Motion on, as explicitly requested. Keep the woodland branches in the background artwork static. Do not add separate swaying branch overlays.

Guest-facing wording stays short and literal. No romantic slogans, story metaphors, heart ornaments or sentimental preambles. The form note is “Formal invitation to follow.” Make this note a clearly visible serif subtitle, and keep Enter information below easy to read. Show Registry with the literal placeholder <link here> on the first invitation section, below Calendar and Directions, until a registry URL is supplied. Do not show a registry block beneath the form or coming-soon wording. Preserve the optional title, Spouse or Fiance's Name (if applicable), required email, mailing address with a ZIP label, and the early attendance question “Are you planning to attend?”. Do not show the Final RSVP to follow note beneath this question.

Keep the response sheet and confirmation-email behavior intact. The previous design is saved at the Git tag checkpoint-2026-11-05.
