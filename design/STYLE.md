# Invitation style

Use one combined invitation view followed by one complete form. All invitation details must be visible together; all form questions must be present together with one Submit button. Use normal page scrolling, with no pinned scenes, scroll-driven fades, chapter progress bar, Next/Back buttons, or repeated scroll prompts.

Carry one continuous watercolor woodland backdrop through the invitation and form, with a soft paper wash that lightens the form for readability. On mobile, size the shared backdrop using a stable large viewport height so browser toolbar changes do not zoom the artwork while scrolling. Separate Name and Mailing address with a fine, muted line (vertical on desktop, horizontal on phones). Keep the warm paper, Columbia Blue `#B9D9EB`, navy text, fine blue rules, and readable form text. Names, date, time and venue use restrained serif typography. Keep the existing calendar and directions buttons comfortably sized.

Use the nighttime waterfront watercolor portrait. The graduation portrait was rejected because only one person wore a gown. Keep the artwork free of tables, chairs, candles and banquet decorations. Do not add a top-left monogram or crown ornament. The favicon stays S&E.

Preserve the existing butterflies and petals: the same illustrations, scattered areas, paths and timings, with no wing scaling or perspective changes. Petals fall downward. Both continue to animate with Reduce Motion on, as explicitly requested. Keep the woodland branches in the background artwork static. Do not add separate swaying branch overlays.

Guest-facing wording stays short and literal. No romantic slogans, story metaphors, heart ornaments or sentimental preambles. The form note is “Formal invitation to follow.” Preserve the optional title, Spouse or Fiance's Name (if applicable), required email, mailing address and early attendance question.

Keep the response sheet and confirmation-email behavior intact. The previous design is saved at the Git tag checkpoint-2026-11-05.
